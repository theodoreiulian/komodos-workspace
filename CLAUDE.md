# Project instructions — The Komodos / Wharton Investment Competition 2026–27

## Context you need every session
- Read `README.md`, then `00-competition/competition-brief.md`. Those are
  authoritative. `01-research/` is our accumulated knowledge.
- Current strategic state lives in `99-admin/decision-log.md`. Read it before
  proposing anything; we may already have ruled an idea in or out.

## Hard constraints that bite
- **Trading:** max 200 trades; stocks priced ≥ $5 only; ETFs OK; Treasury bonds
  from US/UK/DE/FR/IT/NL only. No margin, no shorting, no crypto, no derivatives,
  no stock-secured debt. $25 commission per stock trade, $10 per bond trade.
- **IPS is 50 words (pitch) + 500 words (statement)**, Times New Roman 12pt,
  double-spaced, 1" margins, 3 pages max including title page, PDF ≤ 5 MB.
  **No graphics, charts, images, footnotes or citations permitted in the IPS.**
- **Trading Notes Analysis:** exactly 3 notes, each reproduced verbatim from
  WInS, each with a reflection of **100 words or fewer**.
- After Nov 6 the strategy is frozen. We may not redesign it in hindsight.

## Style
- Plain language beats jargon. A 2025 finale judge explicitly told teams to
  "fight against" complex terminology; the best solutions are straightforward.
- Authentic team voice. The rubric has a whole criterion for it.
- Never write a number we cannot trace to a script or a cited source.

## Do not
- Draft finished deliverable prose that is passed off as the students' own work.
  Produce structure, analysis, numbers, options and critique; the students write
  the submitted text in their own voice. This is a competition rule, not a
  preference.
