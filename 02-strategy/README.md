# Strategy

**Empty by design.** As of 16 September 2026 no strategic decisions have been
taken — the team is in the exploration phase.

Candidate directions and their counter-arguments live in
`../01-research/differentiation-angles.md`. Decisions get recorded in
`../99-admin/decision-log.md` as they are made.

## What will live here
- `investment-thesis.md` — the central idea, once chosen
- `asset-allocation.md` — sleeve structure and target weights
- `glidepath.md` — the rule for how composition changes into and after 2033
- `reserve-design.md` — operating reserve: size, composition, evolution
- `facility-contribution.md` — the recommendation and the co-sponsor range

## The test any strategy must pass
1. Can it be stated in one sentence a judge would repeat back?
2. Does it fund the ten payments with a defensible definition of certainty?
3. Does it explain how the portfolio changes over time, as a **rule**?
4. Is it specific to Laura — or could it have been written about anyone?
5. Can it survive being frozen on 6 November and evaluated on 4 December?
