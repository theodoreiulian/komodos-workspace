# Strategy

**Thesis adopted 23 September 2026:** engineer the funding certainty rather than
estimate it — a maturity-matched Treasury sleeve pays the residency, a separate
growth sleeve funds the facility gift. See `investment-thesis.md`.

Candidate directions and their counter-arguments live in
`../01-research/differentiation-angles.md` (we adopted **Angle B**). Decisions
get recorded in `../99-admin/decision-log.md` as they are made.

## The test any strategy must pass
1. Can it be stated in one sentence a judge would repeat back?
2. Does it fund the ten payments with a defensible definition of certainty?
3. Does it explain how the portfolio changes over time, as a **rule**?
4. Is it specific to Laura — or could it have been written about anyone?
5. Can it survive being frozen on 6 November and evaluated on 4 December?
