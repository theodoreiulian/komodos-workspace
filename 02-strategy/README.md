# Strategy

**Thesis in force (decided 30 Sep 2026):** lock in all ten payments at the start
with a Treasury ladder (the **Promise Portfolio**); invest the rest for growth
(the **Gift Portfolio**); in Jan 2031 lock the bottom of the co-sponsor range
too (the **promise lock**). Decision: `../99-admin/decision-log.md` (30 Sep).
Plain-language brief: `../06-team/briefings/2026-09-30-lock-in-the-promise.md`.
Still open: Gift Portfolio contents (Q4, 7 Oct); glidepath and lock size (Q5/Q6,
16 Oct).

Evidence to decide from: `../01-research/` (especially
`differentiation-angles.md`) and `../03-modeling/`. Open questions:
`../99-admin/decision-log.md`.

## What will live here once decided
- `investment-thesis.md` — the central idea, and the decision it came from
- `asset-allocation.md` — how the money is split and what each part is for
- `reserve-design.md` — the operating reserve: size, what it holds, how it changes
- `glidepath.md` — the rule for how the mix changes before and after 2033
- `facility-contribution.md` — the recommended gift and the co-sponsor range

These are working documents that record decisions and reasoning. They are
**not** submission text — the students write that.

## The test any strategy must pass
1. Can it be said in one sentence a judge would repeat back?
2. Does it fund the ten payments with a definition of certainty we can defend?
3. Does it say how the portfolio changes over time, as a **rule**?
4. Is it specific to Laura — or could it have been written about anyone?
5. Does it survive being frozen on 6 November and judged on 4 December?
