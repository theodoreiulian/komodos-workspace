**Question (Q2/Q3):** how should Laura's portfolio make sure the ten $50,000
payments (1 Jan 2033–2042) are funded "with a high degree of certainty", and how
much of it should be locked in, when?

**Proposal:**
1. **Promise portfolio.** At the start of 2027, lock in all ten payments with a
   cash-flow-matched ladder: US T-bonds maturing 2036–2041 and iShares iBonds
   Term Treasury funds IBTO/IBTP/IBTQ (Dec 2033/34/35). The bonds' coupons
   collected before 2033 pay the 2033 bill. Cost at today's prices ≈ 100–102% of
   Laura's first $300,000 contribution.
2. **Gift portfolio.** Everything else (≈ $140k–160k, mostly the 2028
   contribution) is invested for growth. Contents to be decided separately
   (modelled here as 100% stocks).
3. **Promise lock.** On 1 Jan 2031, when Laura tells co-sponsors her expected
   gift range, 75% of the gift portfolio moves into 2-year Treasuries maturing
   before 1 Jan 2033. The bottom of the range she quotes is that locked amount.
4. **WInS implementation.** The $300,000 WInS account represents the whole
   $450,000 plan at 2/3 scale: ≈ $205,000 ladder (9 trades), ≈ $95,000 growth.
