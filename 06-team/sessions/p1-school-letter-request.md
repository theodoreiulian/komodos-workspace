## The message

**Subject:** A signed school letter for the Wharton investment competition

Hello [advisor's name],

As you know, our team, **The Komodos** ([name 1], [name 2], [name 3], [name 4], [name 5]),
is taking part in the **Wharton Global High School Investment Competition
2026–27**, run by the Wharton School of the University of Pennsylvania. Thank you
again for being our advisor.

Wharton asks every team to send **official documentation from its school** with
the Final Report. It's how the organisers confirm that each team is a genuine
team from a real school, taking part with the school's permission. It isn't
graded, but without it our entry isn't complete.

**What it needs to be:**
- a short letter on **Lycée International letterhead** (the school's official
  paper), **signed** by someone in the school's management (for example the
  proviseur or the head of the American Section);
- it should confirm that:
  1. the Lycée gives **permission** for The Komodos to take part in the
     competition;
  2. the five of us are **students enrolled** at the Lycée;
  3. you are a **member of staff** at the Lycée and our team's advisor.

**What it doesn't need:** anything about our investment work, results or
grades. A few lines is enough.

**Format and timing:** a **scanned PDF** of the signed letter is fine. The final
deadline is **4 December**, but could we have it **by the end of October**, so
it isn't a rush alongside the report?

If it helps, I can send the office our five full names exactly as they appear on
the competition roster.

Thank you,
[your name], Team Leader, The Komodos
