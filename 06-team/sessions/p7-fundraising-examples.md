# P7 — Collect 3 real fundraising examples

**Who:** one member. **Due:** Fri 9 Oct. **Time:** about 1 hour.
**Output:** `06-team/fundraising-examples.md`

## Why
The Final Report must include part of Laura's fundraising materials: the message
that tells possible co-sponsors how much she expects to give to the building. It
is graded under criterion 5 ("communicates Laura's potential facility
contribution and investment uncertainty clearly and credibly to prospective
co-sponsors"). We will write it better if we've studied how real organisations
ask for money.

## What to find
Three real web pages where an **arts residency, artists' space or cultural
centre** asks donors or sponsors to fund a **building or a new space**. Aim for:
- at least one in **Taiwan or elsewhere in Asia** (search ideas: "artist
  residency Taiwan", "Taipei Artist Village", "Treasure Hill Artist Village",
  "capital campaign art center", "artist residency new building campaign"),
- at least one that mentions **how much the founder or organisation is putting in
  themselves**.

## For each one, write down
1. Link, organisation, country, date if shown.
2. **The ask**: how much, and for what.
3. **Their own money**: do they say how much the founder or organisation is
   giving? How do they say it: an exact number, a range, a percentage?
4. **Honesty about uncertainty**: do they admit anything could go wrong or fall
   short? How?
5. **One sentence that convinced you**, and why it worked. You may quote it,
   since this is research notes, not a submission.
6. **One thing that put you off.**

## Then answer, in 3–4 lines of your own
If Laura tells co-sponsors "I will give between $X and $Y", what did these
examples teach you about how to say it so they believe her?
