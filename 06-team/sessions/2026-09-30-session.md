## 1:05–1:25 — P5: Try to break the brief (20 min)

**The idea:** a judge at the semifinal will try to find the weak spot. Find it
first.

**Step 1 — alone (10 min).** Each person writes **two** questions:
1. **The hardest question a judge could ask** about this decision that **you
   couldn't answer** right now.
2. **One thing in the brief you didn't understand**: a word, a number, a step.

Good questions are specific. ❌ "Is this too safe?" ✅ "If Laura's stocks
crash in 2030, what does she tell co-sponsors in 2031?"

To get you started, look at:
- "What we gave up": do you accept why it lost?
- "What would change my mind": is it realistic that this happens by 6 Nov?
- The worked example: can you redo it on your own?
- Every number: is it a fact, a market price, our choice, or a guess?

**Step 2 — together (10 min).** Read the questions out. Any question someone
else *can* answer, they answer out loud (that's practice for the semifinal).
Write the ones **nobody** can answer into `06-team/questions-2026-09-30.md`:

```
# Questions on the 30 Sep brief

| # | Question | Asked by | Could anyone answer? |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 |  |  | No |
```

Claude answers every "No" question before Friday. The ones that expose a real
gap go into the next brief. If a question shows the decision is **wrong**,
Claude will say so and change it.

## P3 — Writing the first Trading Notes (for Friday)

Friday's trades are **the Promise Portfolio**: 9 purchases, listed in the brief
(section 5). **Every trade needs its own Trading Note, written by us.** Notes are
copied word for word into the Trading Notes Analysis on 23 Oct, so they are
graded, submitted work. Claude will not write them.

**Split:** 9 notes ÷ 4 people. Suggested: person A, the 3 funds (IBTO, IBTP,
IBTQ); B, T-bonds 2036 + 2037; C, 2038 + 2039; D, 2040 + 2041.

**What Wharton asks for** (Competition Guide, word for word): the note "should
capture the reasoning behind the decision, including its alignment with your
strategy, the supporting research or analysis, and its expected role in growth,
liquidity, risk management, or future funding."

**Wharton's own example note** (Trading Notes Analysis requirements, word for
word, 63 words):
> "We are purchasing shares of an intermediate-term U.S. Treasury bond ETF to
> reduce portfolio volatility and begin preparing for Laura's future operating
> commitment. The position provides income and greater stability than equities,
> although interest-rate changes may affect its value. This trade supports our
> plan to balance continued growth with reliable future cash flows as the
> residency funding date approaches."

**Our checklist** (our house rule, built from the two quotes above). Every note
covers all five:

| # | The note must say… | Where to find it |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **What** we are buying (name, end date) | Brief §5, order table |
| 2 | **Its role**: which of Laura's payments it pays | Brief §5, "Pays for" column |
| 3 | **The research or analysis behind it**: one number or fact that justifies it (e.g. its yield, or that no US bond ends in that year) | Brief §2 and §2e, Primers A/C |
| 4 | **The risk it accepts**, honestly (Wharton's example does this) | Brief §5, "Risk to name" column |
| 5 | **How it fits the strategy** | Brief §1 |

⚠️ **The brief's wording is Claude's. Don't copy phrases from it into a note.**
Read the facts, close the brief, and write the note from memory in your own
words. The notes are submitted work.

**Rules:**
- Your own words. Short sentences. No jargon without a quick explanation.
- About 50–80 words each (our house rule; Wharton's example is 63). **On Friday,
  check the maximum length of the WInS note box first** and keep under it.
- No two notes should read like copies. Each fund or bond has its own job and its
  own risk, so say what's specific to it.
- Put the drafts in `04-portfolio/trading-notes-drafts.md` by **Friday 12:00**.
  Claude's `rules-auditor` checks them for the five parts and for the rules
  (it doesn't rewrite them). Fix anything flagged, then enter the trades after
  15:30 French time. Copy each note into WInS **and** into
  `04-portfolio/trading-notes.md` at the same moment, and fill in
  `04-portfolio/trade-log.md`.
- **No extra orders without a note.** If a limit order doesn't fill, or fills
  only partly, don't place a new order until its note is written. Every order
  uses one of our 200 trades.
