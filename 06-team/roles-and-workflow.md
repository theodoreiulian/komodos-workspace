## Roles

Wharton's FAQ says teams "should assign different roles to members," and the
rubric rewards demonstrated teamwork. Roles are about *ownership*, not silos —
every investment decision is a team decision, and the FAQ is explicit that trades
"should be a team decision."

| Role | Owns | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Team Leader** | Sole point of contact with Wharton; submits every deliverable via SurveyMonkey Apply | ⚠️ Must be ≥16 on 28 Sep 2026. Changing leader after that date needs approval. Must opt in to programme communications. |
| **Liability & fixed income** | The operating reserve: ladder design, bond selection, reserve sizing, glidepath | The technical core of the case |
| **Growth & research** | Equity sleeve: screening, theses, sector and geographic analysis | Where individual stock work lives |
| **Modelling & risk** | Python models, Monte Carlo, scenario and sensitivity analysis, all numbers in deliverables | Guardian of "every number is reproducible" |
| **Writing & client communication** | Deliverable drafting, word-count discipline, the co-sponsor fundraising draft, team voice | The rubric has two criteria that are mostly about this |

With four students, one person carries two roles — most naturally Team Leader +
one substantive role. **Everyone writes.** The Final Report must sound like a
team, and all members must present at semifinals/finale if we get there.

## Advisor

⚠️ Our teacher advisor **may not make decisions for us or actively participate in
trading or strategy**. They register the team, act as a point of contact, and
give educational guidance. A parent or finance professional may act as an unpaid
*secondary* advisor. **Any paid advisor, consultant, camp or course claiming to
teach this competition = disqualification.**

## Rhythm

| Cadence | What |
|---|---|
| **Weekly, fixed slot** | Strategy meeting. Review positions, decide trades, log decisions. |
| Before every trade | Write the Trading Note *first*. Two people minimum agree. |
| Weekly | Update `99-admin/decision-log.md` — including things we rejected |
| Fortnightly | Re-read the evaluation criteria. Ask: which criterion did we advance? |

### Milestones
- **by 25 Sep** — practice window closes. Answer the WInS instrument questions
  (open question #1). Everyone has logged in at least once.
- **28 Sep** — trading opens. Do not rush. Early trades are the ones most likely
  to end up in the Trading Notes Analysis.
- **9 Oct** — roster due. ⚠️ Collect names, unique emails, birthdates, graduation
  years **now**. Also request school letterhead documentation this month.
- **23 Oct** — Trading Notes Analysis. Shortlist candidate notes from ~15 Oct.
- **6 Nov** — IPS. ⚠️ Strategy freezes. Draft from ~20 Oct; the 500-word limit
  needs many passes.
- **9 Nov** — Final Report requirements published. Read the day they land.
- **4 Dec** — Final Report + school documentation.

All deadlines are **5:00 p.m. ET = 11:00 p.m. in France**. No extensions are
granted, ever. Target submission 24 hours early.

## Working principles

1. **Trading Note before trade.** It is the evidence of reasoning, and Wharton
   verifies notes against executed trades.
2. **Disagreement gets logged.** A documented internal debate is exactly the
   "response to challenges" material criterion 4 asks for.
3. **Plain language.** If we cannot explain a position to someone outside the
   team in one sentence, we do not understand it yet.
4. **Few trades.** 200 is a ceiling, not a target. $25 a trade, and discipline is
   itself evidence.
5. **We follow the AI policy exactly.** See `../00-competition/competition-brief.md`
   § 7. Models we build are ours; AI outputs are sources and get cited; AI does
   not write our submissions.
