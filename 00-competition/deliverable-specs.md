# Deliverable specifications — exact requirements

Anything marked ⚠️ will get us screened out if we miss it. The IPS requirements
document is explicit: *"Submissions that do not meet the requirements below will
not be considered for semifinal selection."*

---

## 1. Official Team Roster — due 9 Oct 2026, 5:00 p.m. ET

Required fields:
- Full name, **unique email**, birthday (M/D/Y), anticipated graduation year —
  for **every student**
- Official team name (**The Komodos**) — must match what we use on the IPS title page
- School country, school city (school state for US schools only)
- Official school website URL

⚠️ Roster locks on submission. Members may not be changed afterwards without
written advisor approval, and team size must stay within 4–6.

## 2. Trading Notes Analysis — due 23 Oct 2026, 5:00 p.m. ET

**Purpose:** demonstrate how our investment decisions reflected the strategy we
were developing — our approach to growth, risk, liquidity, funding reliability,
financial flexibility and future cash-flow needs.

**Format**
- Select **exactly three (3)** Trading Notes from trades **executed in WInS**.
- ⚠️ Include each Trading Note **exactly as it appears in WInS**. Wharton
  verifies this against the platform: *"Yes, we will verify this."*
- For each, a reflection of **100 words or fewer** covering:
  1. why the team made the decision,
  2. how it aligned with the overall investment strategy,
  3. how it supported the client's goals, funding needs, or risk considerations.
- Submitted via a SurveyMonkey Apply form.

**Wharton's own example of a good Trading Note (quoted verbatim):**
> "We are purchasing shares of an intermediate-term U.S. Treasury bond ETF to
> reduce portfolio volatility and begin preparing for Laura's future operating
> commitment. The position provides income and greater stability than equities,
> although interest-rate changes may affect its value. This trade supports our
> plan to balance continued growth with reliable future cash flows as the
> residency funding date approaches."

Read that closely. It is ~65 words and it does five things: names the instrument,
names the *role* in the portfolio, names the *benefit*, **names the risk it
accepts**, and ties back to the strategy. That risk sentence is the part most
teams will omit. Every note we write follows this five-part shape.

**Explicitly not expected at this stage:** the final operating-reserve size,
long-term projections, the facility contribution, or the co-sponsor
communication. Those belong to the Final Report.

**Useful licence:** the three notes *need not* correspond to positions still held
at the end. A trade we later reversed can be excellent evidence — and a note that
honestly says "this tested our assumption and we changed our mind" speaks
directly to criterion 4.

## 3. Investment Policy Statement — due 6 Nov 2026, 5:00 p.m. ET ⚠️⚠️

The single most constrained document of the competition.

### Structure — 3 pages maximum

**Page 1 — title page (1 page max), must contain:**
- Official team name (identical to roster)
- Finalised team member names, formatted **First Name, Last Initial**
- WInS username

**Pages 2–3 — Investment Strategy (2 pages max):**
- **Investment Strategy Elevator Pitch — maximum 50 words**
- **Investment Policy Statement — maximum 500 words**

### ⚠️ Formatting, non-negotiable
| Item | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Font | Times New Roman |
| Size | 12-point |
| Spacing | Double-spaced |
| Margins | 1 inch, all sides |
| File | PDF, ≤ 5 MB, via SurveyMonkey Apply |
| **Forbidden** | graphics, charts, images, attachments, external links, footnotes, formal citations |

Sources need not be cited in the IPS; they must appear in the Final Report.

### What the 500 words must establish
- What we are trying to accomplish for the client
- Why the strategy is appropriate for her and her goals
- How it balances growth, liquidity, funding reliability, risk and financial
  flexibility
- How it will guide portfolio construction and decisions **as her needs change**
- How it protects the required operating payments while supporting a responsible
  facility contribution
- Our strategic approach to: preparing for the operating commitment, managing the
  **operating reserve's asset composition over time**, determining a responsible
  facility contribution, and preserving flexibility

These need **not** be addressed as separate headed sections — Wharton says the
goal is "a clear, cohesive and well-supported investment strategy."

### What the IPS must NOT contain
Final operating-reserve calculations, detailed portfolio projections, a final
facility-contribution range, the draft co-sponsor communication, or descriptions
of individual investments. It is about the **decision framework**, not the
arithmetic and not the holdings.

### The strategic point about the word limit
500 double-spaced words is roughly 1.7 pages of prose — about 3½ minutes of
speech. It is far too short to describe a portfolio. It is exactly long enough to
state one thesis, its rationale, three or four governing principles, and the rule
that changes the portfolio over time. **The word limit is telling us what kind of
document to write.** Budget it deliberately, e.g.:

| Element | Words |
|---|---|
| The thesis / central idea | ~60 |
| Why it fits Laura specifically | ~90 |
| Risk, funding reliability, how we define "high certainty" | ~110 |
| Portfolio construction & diversification principles | ~90 |
| The glidepath rule: how composition changes into and after 2033 | ~100 |
| Facility contribution & flexibility principle | ~50 |

⚠️ **After this deadline the strategy is final, trading ends, and the portfolio
freezes.** We may not revise the strategy in the Final Report.

## 4. Final Report + School Documentation — due 4 Dec 2026, 5:00 p.m. ET

⚠️ Detailed requirements are **published on 9 November** — five days after the
IPS is frozen and 25 days before the deadline. Plan for a tight, intense build.

Known scope: evaluate how we implemented the IPS strategy, and present analysis
and recommendations for
- the **operating commitment** (reserve size, initial composition, how the
  composition should change as payments approach and are made, our definition of
  "a high degree of funding certainty," how we evaluated it, and the assumptions
  behind it),
- the **facility contribution** (how much Laura can responsibly give, and why),
- **financial flexibility** (how much she should preserve),
- **communication with co-sponsors** — including a **drafted portion of Laura's
  fundraising materials** describing the potential contribution, and a
  **recommended dollar range with a stated confidence level**,
all "across varying outcomes."

Citations and supporting evidence are required here (unlike the IPS), including
citation of any AI-generated material.

**School documentation:** official letterhead confirming the team has permission
to participate and that students and advisor belong to the school. Submitted as a
separate file. ⚠️ Request it from the Lycée in **October**, not late November.
