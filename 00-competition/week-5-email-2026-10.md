## Text of the email

Week 5: Wharton Global High School Investment Competition

Congratulations! You have reached the halfway point of the competition. Keep up the good work.

Your team's next deliverable is the Investment Policy Statement (IPS). Remember, the IPS will now be submitted as a form on SurveyMonkey Apply. You will not submit a PDF. Review the updated IPS instructions.

Check out our Instagram page, @whartonyouth, to see some of the teams competing!

A FEW IMPORTANT REMINDERS

Team rosters are final. Teams may not add members or change team leaders.

Before you send a ticket, please try to work through your issue with your teammates and advisor. Most questions can be answered by reading the information that we have already provided.

This is a team competition. A successful team works together.

The following investment types are permitted for modeling both of Laura's contributions and for use in your Investment Policy Statement and Final Report.
- Any stock available on WInS.
- Any Exchange-Traded Fund (ETF) available on WInS.
- Any Government/Treasury Bond available on WInS.

The following are NOT permitted:
- Margin trading
- Short selling
- Stock-secured debt
- Cryptocurrency
- Derivatives, including options, futures, swaps, etc.
- Individual TIPS (Treasury Inflation-Protected Securities)
- Individual STRIPS (Separate Trading of Registered Interest and Principal of Securities)
- Any investment other than the approved investments listed above.

When it comes to submitting your deliverables, we strongly encourage your team not to wait until the last possible minute.

Start working on your School Documentation now.

TIPS FOR COMPETITION SUCCESS

- Let your investment strategy lead. It connects every part of the competition: from your WInS portfolio and Trading Notes Analysis to your Investment Policy Statement and Final Report.
- Know your client. Review the Client Case Study carefully so you understand Laura's goals. If anything is unclear, discuss it with your advisor.
- Use WInS to demonstrate your strategy. Your WInS portfolio should reflect the strategy your team has developed. Trading ends on November 6 at 5:00 p.m. ET, and your portfolio will be final.
- Do not focus on portfolio performance. You are not evaluated on whether your WInS portfolio gains or loses money. WInS results should not be added to or subtracted from Laura's long-term portfolio projections.
- Explain your decisions. There is no single correct strategy. Use your research and analysis to explain why each investment belongs in your strategy and how it supports Laura's goals.
- Follow the instructions. Review the requirements and guidelines for each deliverable before submitting your work.

IMPORTANT DATES

- Friday, November 6, 5:00 p.m. ET: Investment Policy Statement due and trading ends
- Monday, November 9, 9:00 a.m. ET: Instructions and requirements for the Final Report posted
- Friday, December 4, 5:00 p.m. ET: School documentation and Final Report due
