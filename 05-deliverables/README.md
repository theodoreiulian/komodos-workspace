## Before every submission
Run the checklist at the end of `../00-competition/deliverable-specs.md`. Word
counts verified by script, not by eye. Formatting checked on the **exported PDF**,
not the source document. Submit 24 hours early — deadlines are 11:00 p.m. French
time and **no extensions are granted**.

## Who does what
Claude decides what each deliverable must say, drafts and revises the complete
text, chooses the Trading Notes, and runs the review passes (`judge-panel`,
`rules-auditor`). The students review the material so they can explain and
present it clearly.
